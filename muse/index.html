Muse

Twenty-one oscillators moving through a field of chords.

Instrument

A large sound controlled with a few coordinates.

Muse layers twenty-one wavetable oscillators and animates them with twenty-three low-frequency modulators. X, Y, and Z travel through pitch, waveform, and amplitude relationships while pitch, index, animation, phase modulation, and quantisation shape the larger gesture.

Consonance

Make the harmonic field simpler without making it static.

Muse-XT adds a consonance threshold that filters the X sweep by the least-consonant interval in each chord. At one extreme the full field remains available; at the other, movement is restricted to octave and unison relationships.

Lineage

A clean, independent implementation.

Muse studies a rare multi-oscillator hardware concept through independently written DSP for VCV Rack, MetaModule, and Daisy. Its clean-history public repository contains only redistributable project code and assets. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the designers or manufacturers of related hardware.

The VCV and Daisy versions are works in progress. Back up important patches, expect sound and control behaviour to evolve, and treat cross-platform packages as release candidates until acceptance testing is complete.
