Inflection

Four folding channels whose mirrors move with the signal.

Instrument

Folding that distinguishes movement from position.

Each channel reflects the signal between two mirrors. Spacing sets their base distance, Offset moves them together, and Motion narrows their separation in response to input slope. Slow contours and fast edges can therefore enter different folding regimes at the same level.
