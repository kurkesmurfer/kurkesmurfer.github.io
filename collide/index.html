Collide

A lock-in amplifier taken apart and made patchable.

Architecture

Six views into one unusual signal path.

Collide turns the functional blocks of a musical lock-in amplifier into separate VCV modules. Use them as one analysis-and-resynthesis chain, or take the bandwidth filter, quadrature oscillator, phase network, demodulator, and analogue-computing ideas into unrelated patches.

Amp→Bandf→Hilbert→Demod→Polar

Preview status

Ready for platform testing.

Release-candidate packages have been built for Apple Silicon macOS, Intel macOS, Windows x64, and Linux x64. Hardware and cross-platform acceptance testing is still in progress.

Module family

Patch the whole instrument or borrow a block.

Amp

Stepped preamplification, differential input, AC coupling, and voltage-controlled gain.

Bandf

A pingable variable-bandwidth filter with centre, width, and resonance control.

Quadosc

A through-zero quadrature oscillator with sine/cosine outputs and pitch tracking.

Demod

Quadrature multiplication, time-constant filtering, and sum/difference outputs.

Hilbert

A wideband phase-splitting network with in-phase and quadrature outputs.

Polar

Converts I/Q signals into magnitude and phase control voltages.

Lineage

An independent digital interpretation.

The project is inspired by the signal architecture and musical possibilities of the Joranalogue Audio Design × Hainbach Collide 4 and by historical lock-in amplifiers. Its software, DSP implementation, panel system, and separation into six modules are independent Kurkesmurfer work. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Joranalogue Audio Design or Hainbach.

This is a digital VCV instrument, not an electrical model of the analogue hardware. Expect its own gain structure, nonlinearities, limits, and sound.
