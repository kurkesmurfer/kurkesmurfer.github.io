9 August 2026 · Collide + Muse

Two new fields of sound

Two instrument families have reached the point where their scope, status, and unfinished edges can be shown in public.

Collide

Collide turns lock-in-amplifier ideas into six focused VCV Rack modules: a preamplifier, band-pass filter, quadrature oscillator, demodulator, Hilbert network, and polar converter. Dark and light panels are complete, and release-candidate packages build for macOS Intel and Apple Silicon, Windows x64, and Linux x64.

Muse

Muse is a many-voice drone instrument built around 21 oscillators, 23 modulation sources, and a 61-position chord space. Muse-XT adds consonance filtering on VCV Rack and MetaModule. Daisy currently keeps the core Muse control set; its next hardware pass will smooth external X, Y, and Z control so slow CV movement remains free of audible stepping.

Release posture

Both projects are licensed GPL-3.0-or-later and presented as independent implementations. Muse now has a clean-history public source repository. Installable packages remain release candidates until reproducible tags and cross-platform acceptance testing are complete.
