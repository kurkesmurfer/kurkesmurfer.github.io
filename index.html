Quadrature spectral tools

Homodyne

Six patchable building blocks explore the lock-in amplifier as an instrument: amplification, variable-bandwidth filtering, quadrature oscillation and demodulation, phase splitting, and polar computation.

Explore Homodyne Source

Twenty-one oscillator instrument

Muse

Muse is a dense stereo drone voice animated by a field of oscillators and low-frequency modulators. Muse-XT extends it with a consonance control that reduces the broad harmonic space to progressively simpler interval families.

Explore Muse Source

Motion-sensitive wavefolding

Inflection

Four independent mirror folders make the fold spacing respond to signal motion. Slow shapes remain broad while fast edges can drive into tighter, denser reflections.

Explore Inflection Source

Ninety-nine-stage phase laboratory

MultiPhaser

A stereo all-pass network with continuously addressed stages, sixteen selectable tap pairs, CV and clock scanning, and a compact variable-Q phase voice.

Explore MultiPhaser Source
