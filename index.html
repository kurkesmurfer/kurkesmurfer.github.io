Quadrature spectral tools

Homodyne

Six patchable building blocks explore the lock-in amplifier as an instrument: amplification, variable-bandwidth filtering, quadrature oscillation and demodulation, phase splitting, and polar computation.

Explore Homodyne

Twenty-one oscillator instrument

Muse

Muse is a dense stereo drone voice animated by a field of oscillators and low-frequency modulators. Muse-XT extends it with a consonance control that reduces the broad harmonic space to progressively simpler interval families.

Explore Muse
